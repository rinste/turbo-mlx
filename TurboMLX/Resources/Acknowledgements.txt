Turbo MLX: acknowledgements

Turbo MLX is free software under the MIT License, © 2026 Stefano Rinaldo:

It is built on open-source software; thank you to its authors. The models

own license (Settings → About).

The engine's Swift ports follow these projects:

  mflux: the image families, module for module
    https://github.com/mflux-community/mflux
  ltx-2-mlx: LTX-2.3, video and sound
    https://github.com/dgrauet/ltx-2-mlx
  SenseNova-U1: SenseNova-U1.5, from SenseTime's own code
    https://github.com/OpenSenseNova/SenseNova-U1
  MOSS-Image: MOSS-Image, from OpenMOSS's own pipeline
    https://huggingface.co/OpenMOSS-Team/MOSS-Image-SFT
  Idol: Idol, from Sperid Labs' own code (idol-sdk)
    https://github.com/speridlabs/idol-sdk
  BiRefNet: the cutout of the picture Idol makes an object of
    https://github.com/ZhengPeng7/BiRefNet
  DINOv3: DINOv3 ViT-H+/16, which reads Idol's picture; its weights come under this license
    https://github.com/facebookresearch/dinov3
  mlx-serve: Idol's deformable convolution kernel and its GLB of discs
    https://github.com/ddalcu/mlx-serve
  MiniMax-Music3: MiniMax-Music3; its weights come under this license
    https://huggingface.co/MiniMaxAI/MiniMax-Music3
  mlx-audio: MiniMax-Music3, from its MLX port
    https://github.com/Blaizzy/mlx-audio
  minimax-music3-mlx: MiniMax-Music3: the MLX port mlx-audio's follows
    https://github.com/mikolaj92/minimax-music3-mlx
  diffusers: MiniMax-Music3's prompt, from the official integration
    https://github.com/huggingface/diffusers
  MOSS-TTS: MOSS-SoundEffect, from OpenMOSS's own pipeline (moss_soundeffect_v2)
    https://github.com/OpenMOSS/MOSS-TTS
  ftfy: MOSS-SoundEffect's prompt cleaning, which follows its fixes
    https://github.com/rspeer/python-ftfy
  mlx-vlm: Describe Image's Qwen2.5-VL, from its MLX port
    https://github.com/Blaizzy/mlx-vlm
  mlx-lm: the mixture-of-experts layers and the gated delta rule's kernel
    https://github.com/ml-explore/mlx-lm
  mlx-swift-lm: the mixture-of-experts layers
    https://github.com/ml-explore/mlx-swift-lm
  Pillow: its bicubic and Lanczos resizes, for the pictures edits start from
    https://github.com/python-pillow/Pillow
  tokenizers: the tokenizer, which follows its BPE
    https://github.com/huggingface/tokenizers

The engine contains SeedVR2's fixed text embedding (pos_emb), from
